Nuer games

Two traditional seed-sowing board games, made for phones. Play the computer at three levels, or pass the device to a friend.

Yiët

2 players · 4 rows of 13 pits · 52 seeds each

  • Pick one of your pits with two or more seeds and sow them around your two rows.
  • If your last seed lands in a pit that already has seeds, you take them all and keep sowing.
  • In the main game, ending in your inner row captures both opposite pits, and you sow the winnings.
  • The first two turns are practice turns with no captures. You lose when you cannot move.
Play Yiët

Luuth

2 players · 2 rows of 7 pits · 1 seed per pit

  • Each player first moves one seed from their side to any pit.
  • Then choose a pit and a direction, left or right, and sow one seed per pit.
  • If your last seed lands alone in your own empty pit, you capture the seeds in the pit opposite.
  • When one side runs out, each player keeps the seeds left on their side. Most seeds wins.
Play Luuth
